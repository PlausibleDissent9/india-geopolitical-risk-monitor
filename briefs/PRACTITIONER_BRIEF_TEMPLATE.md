# IGRM Practitioner Brief — monthly template (V18)

*One page, sent monthly to the registered think-tank roster. Every
number drawn from published payloads, checkable at the cited URLs on
the stated data date. The attribution and what-this-does-not-say
blocks ship with every issue, verbatim from the NEF Risk Watch
format (nef/RISK_WATCH_TEMPLATE.md). All sends are the founder's.*

**[MONTH IN ONE LINE]** — the composite's month: mean, range, and
whether month-end sits above or below the trailing-two-year median.

**[THE MONTH'S EPISODES]** — every detected episode (channel, dates,
peak), each linked to its receipts; episodes are the month's story,
not the daily wiggle.

**[PERSISTENT VS PASSING]** — which channels' 7-day headline held
elevated for 2+ weeks vs single-day tape events that reversed;
band-separated moves flagged as such.

**[EVIDENCE DEPTH]** — articles enumerated behind the loudest
channel; tier-1/2 share; source breadth (domains); tone axis where
published.

**[INSTRUMENT HEALTH]** — the morning-contract hit rate and anything
in the corrections ledger this month, stated plainly. The reliability
record is part of the product.

**[WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY]** — salience is press attention, not risk;
no forecast is implied, none is made.
