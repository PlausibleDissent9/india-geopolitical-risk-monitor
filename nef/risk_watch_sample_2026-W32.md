# IGRM Risk Watch — sample item, week of 2026-08-03

*A worked sample for the NEF pilot, built entirely from payloads
published at igrm.in on 2026-08-05 (data date 2026-08-04). Numbers
in this sample will be stale by the time you read it; the format is
the deliverable.*

---

**India's geopolitical salience is running hot: the IGRM composite
stands at 75.1** — global press coverage of India's five geopolitical
channels is louder than on three-quarters of days in each channel's
own trailing two years (95% sampling band 65.9–81.1).

**What moved.** Shipping & chokepoints remains the loudest channel at
95.0 (band 93.1–96.4), its second consecutive week above 90 on Red
Sea and freight-lane coverage. The Pakistan / western border channel
sits at 91.4 (band 86.8–93.3) on Article 370 anniversary coverage.
The China / eastern border channel printed the week's largest
day-move, but its sampling bands overlap across the two days
(45.0–90.5 on Aug 4): that move is **not distinguishable from
sampling noise**, and IGRM's own front page says so. US & trade
policy is the quiet channel at 35.8 (band 26.6–45.9).

**The evidence.** Behind Aug 5's shipping number: 120 matched articles
in the day's scored sample, receipts tier-sorted at
igrm.in/receipts.html.

**What this does not say.** Salience is press attention, not risk; no
forecast is implied, and none is made anywhere in IGRM.
