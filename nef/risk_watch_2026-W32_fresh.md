**India's geopolitical salience eased to 58.4 on the IGRM composite**
(95% sampling band 53.6–68.4) — global press coverage of India's five
geopolitical channels was louder than on roughly six in ten days of
each channel's own trailing two years. The one-day drop from 75.1
sits inside overlapping sampling bands; the channel moves below do
not.

**What moved.** Two declines are band-separated — real beyond
sampling noise. The China / eastern border channel fell from 74.3 to
4.5 (bands 45.0–90.5 vs 0.4–36.4) as the diplomacy-led coverage wave
of early August receded. Shipping & chokepoints stepped down from
95.0 to 83.2 (93.1–96.4 vs 77.5–88.4), ending its run above 90. The
Pakistan / western border channel stayed hot at 90.2 (band
85.7–92.7), statistically unchanged. Gulf & energy held at 78.5 in a
tight band (77.7–79.3): 709 matched articles in the day's corpus,
"Strait of Hormuz" in 179 matched headlines. US & trade policy
remained the quiet channel at 35.6.

**The evidence.** Behind these numbers: 709 matched gulf articles and
120 scored shipping articles in the day's own corpus, plus retrieved
wire originals, tier-sorted per channel at igrm.in/receipts.html.

**What this does not say.** Salience is press attention, not risk; no
forecast is implied, and none is made anywhere in IGRM.
