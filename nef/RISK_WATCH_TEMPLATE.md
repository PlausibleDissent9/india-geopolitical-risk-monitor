# IGRM Risk Watch — template for NEF publications

*Prepared for the NEF 30-day internal pilot (approved by Director Jagdip
Rana, August 2026). Format: one item, 150–220 words, weekly or on
episode. Every number is drawn from published IGRM payloads and is
independently checkable at the URLs cited. The attribution and
disclaimer blocks are part of the format and must ship with every item.*

---

## Structure (five slots)

**[HEADLINE]** — the composite and its direction, one line.

**[WHAT MOVED]** — the channel(s) driving it, with scores and, where
published, the 95% sampling band. A move inside overlapping bands is
reported as "not distinguishable from sampling noise" — that sentence
is a feature of the format, not a hedge.

**[THE EVIDENCE]** — one line pointing at receipts: how many matched
articles, what share tier-1/2, with the receipts URL.

**[CONTEXT]** — trailing percentile meaning restated in one clause
("louder than N% of days in that channel's own last two years").

**[WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY]** — one line. Salience is press attention,
not risk, and implies no forecast.

## Attribution block (mandatory, verbatim)

> Source: India Geopolitical Risk Monitor (Ishan Krishna), igrm.in.
> Data CC BY 4.0. IGRM measures press salience — the share of global
> news coverage matching registered channel dictionaries, ranked
> against each channel's own trailing two years. It is not a measure
> of risk and makes no forecasts. Methodology, validation record, and
> per-article receipts: igrm.in/methodology.html, igrm.in/validation.html,
