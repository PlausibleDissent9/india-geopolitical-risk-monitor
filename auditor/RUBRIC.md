# Precision auditor rubric, v1.0.0 (registered 2026-08-03)

Versioned like any instrument; changes are dated amendments. The
question for every sampled article is exactly one: is this article
substantively about the channel's construct, as defined below? Answer
ON, OFF, or ABSTAIN. Headline plus visible summary is the evidence;
never assume beyond them. ABSTAIN when a ruling would require reading
the full text or guessing intent; abstentions route to the human queue
and are excluded from precision, counted separately.

## pakistan_west
ON: India-Pakistan military, border, ceasefire, terrorism-attribution,
or bilateral-crisis coverage; Afghanistan-border security affecting
India. OFF: cricket and sport, entertainment, diaspora human-interest,
trade-only stories without a security frame.

## china_east
ON: India-China border, military, diplomatic-security relations,
strategic competition affecting India's east and north. OFF: China
business/tech stories without an India-security frame, culture,
generic Asia roundups where India-China security is incidental.

## gulf_energy
ON: Gulf-region security, energy-supply risk, tanker and
infrastructure incidents, OPEC decisions framed as supply risk,
Iran-adjacent escalation with energy or India exposure. OFF: routine
price tickers with no risk frame, company earnings, lifestyle Gulf
coverage.

## us_trade
ON: US-India trade policy, tariffs, sanctions with India exposure,
export controls, trade-negotiation coverage. OFF: US domestic politics
without India-trade linkage, immigration human-interest, tech-company
news without a policy frame.

## shipping
ON: maritime chokepoint disruption, shipping-lane security, freight
and reroute coverage, naval escort operations, canal and strait
incidents. OFF: cruise tourism, shipbuilding business news, port
finance stories without a disruption frame, "Suez Canal" as mere
historical reference.
