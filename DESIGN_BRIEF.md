# Freelance design brief — India Geopolitical Risk Monitor (igrm.in)

Post this on Contra, Upwork, or Dribbble. Budget and timeline are
stated so only people who can actually deliver reply.

---

## The project

igrm.in is a live, daily-updating public research instrument: a
geopolitical risk index for India, decomposed into five channels,
published every morning with the source articles behind every number.
It is real infrastructure, not a concept — it has run since 2017 in
data, updates itself daily, and is used by policy practitioners.

I need a designer to take it from competent to editorial-grade.

## What I want

A **design system**, not a one-off mockup:

1. A token set: type scale, spacing rhythm, and a chart-color system
   that works in both light and dark themes.
2. The homepage redesigned: the headline number and five channel rows
   are the hero; they must read instantly on a phone.
3. One evidence-page template redesigned: dense information (source
   articles, tiers, event counts) made scannable.
4. A short usage note explaining the rules so my engineering can apply
   the system to the remaining twenty pages myself.

Deliver in Figma with tokens defined as styles/variables. No code
required — implementation is handled.

## References (the standard I am aiming at)

Financial Times data journalism, The Economist's data pages, and FRED
(fred.stlouisfed.org) for instrument-like clarity. Serious,
information-dense, quiet. Not a startup landing page, not a dashboard
template, no gradients-and-glass.

## Constraints

- Keep the existing identity: dark-first "night paper" feel, serif
  display type (currently Fraunces), sans body (currently Archivo).
  Improve the execution; do not rebrand.
- Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast minimum, and the channel colors must
  stay distinguishable for color-blind readers.
- Mobile is not an afterthought; assume half the readers are on
  phones.
- Everything must be implementable in static HTML and CSS with no
  frameworks and no external assets (the site has a strict CSP and
  self-hosts everything).

## Budget and timeline

USD 800-1000, milestone-based: 40% on accepted direction, 60% on
final delivery. I need the direction within 4 days of starting and
final files within 10 days.

## How to apply

Send: two portfolio pieces that are information-dense (data, editorial,
or financial products — not marketing sites), and one sentence on what
you would change first about igrm.in. I read every reply personally.
