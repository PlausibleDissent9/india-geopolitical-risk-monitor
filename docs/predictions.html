The rules

A prediction enters this archive only through a dated commit made before the outcome is knowable. At its horizon it receives exactly one grade: correct, wrong, or unresolvable, each with a one-paragraph account and links to the evidence. Entries are never edited afterward; corrections of the grading itself would go through the corrections ledger. The index itself predicts nothing (methodology §1); predictions here come from the written analyses, and their scoring measures the author's judgment, not the instrument.
