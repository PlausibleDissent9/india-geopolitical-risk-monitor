What must exist before IGRM may publish canonical event objects

  2. Cross-source resolution with declared independence groups
  3. Role-eligible evidence for every lifecycle transition
  4. Rights-approved source and decision snapshots
  5. Registered executable methods or signed manual decisions
  6. Externally verifiable signed release manifest
  7. Point-in-time revision log and correction blast-radius graph
