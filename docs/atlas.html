Production gate

What must be true before Atlas becomes a real dependency network

The October objective is not to draw more lines. It is to publish a bounded universe whose omissions, rights, freshness and edge accuracy can be audited.

  • Declared universes.Versioned firm, port, commodity, corridor, sector and state frames with inclusion rules and denominators.
  • Rights-approved evidence.Signed source decisions and redistributable lineage for every public edge.
  • Independent edge audit.Precision, recall, staleness and missingness measured against the declared frame.
  • Typed transformations.Only registered, unit-compatible traversal and scenario functions; unsupported chains abstain.
  • Versioned releases.Signed knowledge cutoffs, correction history and exact point-in-time reconstruction.

Atlas is one pillar of the eight-part IGRM system. Its machine foundations use the open evidence standard; its data limitations remain governed by the methodology and corrections record.

Atlas distinguishes observation, association, exposure and hypothetical scenario output. It does not convert any of them into a claim of risk, causation, probability, loss or advice.
