The next orders of magnitude

From coverage matrix to consequence engine

The empty domains are an executable build queue, not teaser copy.

  1. 01Global event and episode ledger

    Typed country episodes, counts, active/closed windows and source disagreement—without calling press aggregates a census.

  2. 02Economic and defence systems

    Macro, AI, resources, weapons, transfers and military activity in separate units and safety classes.

  3. 03India consequence traversal

    Country → policy/event → commodity/route/firm → Indian sector/state paths with complete declared universes.

  4. 04Historical intelligence

    Regimes, structural breaks, archetypes and analogs that expose disanalogies and preserve knowledge cutoffs.

The World State Matrix is a coverage ledger. It does not convert unlike domains into a scalar world ranking and it does not infer absent observations.
