Disagreement is a result

IGRM measures the share of monitored coverage devoted to five India-relevant fault lines. Its comparators measure other things: AI-GPR scores risk intensity in a different newspaper corpus; IMF PortWatch counts ships. When their ranks separate sharply, this page preserves the date, the two readings and a route back to the evidence. It does not decide which instrument is right.

Receipts and reuse

The machine-readable register is divergence_register.json. The AI-GPR rows come from the verbatim registered output; its protocol, frozen script and interpretation are public. Only ranks and aggregates are republished from AI-GPR, never its raw values.

Append-only policy

Rows are never silently deleted. If a source revises, the original row stays and a dated correction is attached. New rows need a named comparator, a frozen sample, a rank rule, a period and a receipt. This keeps the register useful even when a comparison is awkward for IGRM.
