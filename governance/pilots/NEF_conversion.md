# NEF — pilot close and conversion

**Deadline: around 2026-09-05**, when the 30-day internal pilot ends.
Founder time: two emails.

---

## What is actually held today

Nothing on paper. The record is: Director Jagdip Rana approved a 30-day
internal pilot on 2026-08-05, as reported by the founder.

That is testimony, and testimony is not evidence. It cannot support an
adoption claim, and `governance/pilots/instances.json` refuses to record
instances against an institution whose authority is still testimony.

**The first ask is therefore not the conversion. It is the document.**

## Email 1 — now, not at pilot end

Ask Rana (or whoever sent the approval) to forward the original approval,
or send two lines confirming it. Framing matters: this is a request for a
record, not a request for a favour.

> Subject: Quick record of the IGRM pilot approval
>
> Dear Mr Rana,
>
> Could I ask you to forward the note approving the 30-day IGRM pilot, or
> simply reply confirming it? I keep a public record of who authorised
> what, and I would rather cite your own words than my summary of them.
>
> Nothing changes about the pilot either way.
>
> Thank you,
> Ishan

If the answer is no, or nothing comes back, that is informative and gets
recorded as such. It does not get worked around.

## Email 2 — at pilot end

Two things, and they are separable on purpose so a no to one is not a no
to both.

**(a) Attribution.** Permission to state publicly that NEF used IGRM
during a 30-day internal pilot. One sentence, exact wording agreed with
them, no implication of endorsement.

**(b) A short letter.** Three or four sentences on what they used it for
and what they concluded, including anything that did not work.

The second is worth much more than the first, and it is worth being
explicit that criticism is welcome — a letter saying "we found the daily
index too noisy for our purpose but the source receipts useful" is more
credible than a warm one, and more useful to the reviewers.

> Subject: Closing the IGRM pilot — and one honest question
>
> Dear Mr Rana,
>
> The 30-day pilot closes this week. Two things, either of which you are
> free to decline:
>
> First, may I state publicly that NEF used IGRM during an internal pilot?
> I would send you the exact sentence first, and it would not imply NEF
> endorses the index.
>
> Second, would you be willing to write three or four sentences on what you
> used it for and what you concluded? Including what did not work — I am
> being reviewed externally in October and an honest account is worth more
> to me than a flattering one.
>
> Either way, thank you for the month.
>
> Ishan

## What must not happen

- No claim of NEF adoption before the attribution wording is agreed.
- No instance recorded in the pilot ledger without a named user and a
  decision owner confirmed **by NEF**, not inferred.
- A pilot that ends with no conversion is recorded as a completed pilot
  with no conversion. That is a real outcome and it counts in the
  denominator.

## Then what

If (a) lands, the attribution sentence goes into the evidence-to-score
matrix under federation, worth part of 3 points, and nowhere else — it is
not also methodology evidence or product evidence.

If (b) lands, it goes to the two independent reviewers as context on real
institutional use, and to the founder's college applications as the thing
a letter can point at.

If neither lands by 2026-09-14, federation is marked **at risk** in the
execution ledger and engineering continues. It is not a reason to soften
anything.
